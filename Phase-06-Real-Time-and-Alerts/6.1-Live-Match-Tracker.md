## Implementation Notes

- Live UI at `app/live/[matchId]/page.tsx` — animated score, reverse-chronological event timeline with icons and AI comments.
- DB tables: `live_events(match_id, minute, event_type, team_id, player_name, detail, ai_comment)`.
